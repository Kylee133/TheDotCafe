GET TO KNOW US

A little nostalgia.
A lot of heart.

Welcome to The Dot, a cozy neighborhood café located in West Ames, Iowa.

As a woman-owned small business, The Dot combines familiar comfort food with creative, modern twists.

By partnering with local businesses and prioritizing fresh ingredients, The Dot brings the community together through good food and welcoming experiences.
