Our Semester Project

Throughout the semester, our group evaluated The Dot Cafe's online presence and developed recommendations to make its website easier to navigate, strengthen search visibility, and encourage visits.

Our work connects website research with a display advertising campaign.

01 · Website Audit

Evaluating the Website

We assessed the Home, Menu, About, and Contact pages for user interface, user experience, search engine optimization, and performance.

02 · Key Findings

Finding Opportunities

Our audit identified improvements in readability, navigation, calls to action, menu accessibility, metadata, and loading efficiency.

03 · Website Redesign

A Better Experience

We introduced clearer menu and visit buttons, improved text contrast, more intuitive sections, and mobile-friendly layouts while keeping The Dot's nostalgic style.

04 · Display Advertising

Growing Awareness

We designed a cohesive display campaign with audience targeting, benchmark-based planning, four ad formats, and a dedicated landing-page concept.
