Our Marketing Class Project

Making a great café easier to discover.

Throughout the semester, our group evaluated The Dot Cafe's digital presence and developed recommendations to improve the customer experience, website visibility, and marketing effectiveness.

01

Website Audit

Reviewed user interface, user experience, on-site SEO, and website performance across key pages.

02

Recommendations

Identified opportunities to improve readability, menu navigation, calls to action, and page optimization.

03

Display Campaign

Designed a coordinated set of display ads and a dedicated landing-page concept to increase local awareness and menu exploration.

Executive Summary

Our team conducted a website audit of The Dot Cafe's Home, Menu, About, and Contact pages. The website had a consistent identity and generally strong technical performance, but we identified areas for improvement in text contrast, navigation, visible calls to action, meta descriptions, menu organization, and resource efficiency. Building on these findings, we designed a display advertising campaign featuring four ad formats and a focused landing-page experience. Together, these recommendations aim to help potential customers discover The Dot, find what they need more easily, and feel encouraged to visit. This summary can be expanded as our semester project progresses.
