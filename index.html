OUR STORY

A little nostalgia.

As a woman-owned small business, The Dot brings together nostalgic comfort food and modern culinary creativity.

IOWA STATE UNIVERSITY MARKETING PROJECT

Our Semester Project

Throughout the semester, our marketing group has analyzed The Dot Cafe's digital presence and explored ways to improve its website and online marketing strategy.

Our goal is to make the website easier to navigate, improve online visibility, and encourage more customers to discover The Dot.

01 — WEBSITE AUDIT

Evaluating the Website

Our group evaluated the Home, Menu, About, and Contact pages, focusing on user interface, user experience, search engine optimization, and overall performance.

02 — KEY FINDINGS

Identifying Improvements

We identified opportunities to improve readability, navigation, menu organization, calls to action, metadata, and website loading efficiency.

03 — WEBSITE REDESIGN

Improving User Experience

Our redesigned homepage includes clearer navigation, visible menu and visit buttons, improved text contrast, and a mobile-friendly layout while maintaining The Dot's aesthetic.

04 — DISPLAY ADVERTISING

Promoting The Dot Cafe

Our group developed a display advertising campaign with targeting criteria, financial estimates, four creative sizes, and a dedicated landing page concept.

This executive summary will be updated as additional assignments are completed.
